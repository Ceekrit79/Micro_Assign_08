Flowers Grown Over the Years

Welcome to My Garden

Over the past few years, I have enjoyed growing and photographing a variety of colorful flowers in my garden. Each bloom brings different colors, textures, and pollinators to the yard throughout the seasons.

Explore My Blooms

Spring & Early Summer

Delicate blossoms like white columbine and pink foxglove bring soft pastel tones and attract bees and hummingbirds early in the season.

Late Summer Sun Lovers

Bold blooms like California poppies, daylilies, and red-orange sunflowers thrive in warm sunshine and add bright color to the yard.
