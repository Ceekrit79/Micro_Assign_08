Simple Garden Care Tips

What Helps My Flowers Thrive

Keeping garden flowers healthy comes down to matching each plant with the right amount of light, water, and seasonal care:

  • Match sun-loving plants like poppies and sunflowers with bright spots.
  • Give woodland blooms like columbine and foxglove partial afternoon shade.
  • Deadhead faded blossoms to encourage fresh blooms through the season.
