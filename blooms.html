Favorite Garden Blooms

White Columbine

Known for its delicate spurred petals and soft green foliage, the white columbine adds a gentle, airy look to shaded garden corners.

California Poppy

These bright orange cup-shaped flowers open in full sunlight and bring vibrant warm color to borders and garden beds.

Pink Foxglove

Tall stalks lined with tubular pink bells create strong vertical interest and are a favorite stop for visiting bumblebees.

Red-Orange Sunflower

With fiery red and gold petals surrounding a dark seed center, this sunflower stands tall as a bold late-summer centerpiece.
